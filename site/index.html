Tempo Focus

A focus timer for deep work — free Chrome extension

Tempo Focus turns your browser into a deep-work hub. Run structured focus sessions, keep your tasks in one place, and see honest statistics about where your attention actually went.

Install from the Chrome Web Store

What it does

Focus timer that keeps running

Start a session and close the popup — the timer keeps counting in the background and notifies you the moment it completes.

Tasks, with optional Google Tasks sync

Keep a task list beside your timer. If you choose to connect your Google account, your Google Tasks appear in Tempo and completions sync back.

Soundscapes and focus audio

Ambient rain, cafe, river, waves, and campfire loops, plus binaural tones and your own YouTube focus tracks — all playing in the background while you work.

Statistics and streaks

Session history, daily and weekly breakdowns, and streaks, so progress is measured rather than guessed at.

Your data stays yours

Tempo Focus has no backend. Your sessions, tasks, and settings live in your own browser storage. We operate no servers that receive your data, we do not sell it, and we show no ads. Read the full Privacy Policy for details on exactly what is accessed and why.
